# Moyo Heart Compass Care

Based on the MoyoDoc Pitch Deck, Product Requirements Document (PRD), and Enhanced Concept Note, I recommend positioning the website as a specialist-led hypertension care platform, not another health-tracking app.

The homepage should speak to two audiences simultaneously:

1. Patients: People living with hypertension, people who have been diagnosed but struggle to keep their blood pressure under control, and people who do not know their risk.
2. Healthcare providers: Cardiologists, internists and other qualified cardiovascular practitioners who want better visibility of their patients' health between clinic visits.

The central message should be:

Your blood pressure needs attention beyond the clinic. MoyoDoc keeps you and your cardiovascular specialist connected.

The design below is structured as a ready-to-use website creation brief, with actual website copy, navigation, CTAs, visual direction, page sections and interaction instructions. It follows the two-door, one-clinical-loop model in your concept note and deck.&#x20;

# MoyoDoc homepage — design and content specification

# MOYODOC — WEBSITE HOMEPAGE

## Complete design and content brief for Lovable / Figma

### 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Brand:** MoyoDoc\
**Tagline:** Better blood pressure care. Beyond the clinic.\
**Market:** Kenya (initial launch), with an African expansion vision.\
**Website type:** Healthcare technology platform / patient acquisition / healthcare provider onboarding.\
**Primary objective:** Convert website visitors into patient registrations and cardiovascular risk assessments.\
**Secondary objective:** Recruit qualified cardiovascular healthcare professionals and practices into the MoyoDoc network.

### 2. BRAND POSITIONING

MoyoDoc is a specialist-led cardiovascular care platform focused on continuous hypertension management.

It connects patients with cardiovascular specialists and helps maintain continuity of care between clinic visits through:

- Cardiovascular health intake and risk stratification.
- Guided matching with enlisted cardiovascular specialists.
- Home blood pressure monitoring.
- Medication adherence tracking.
- Weekly symptom check-ins.
- Structured alerts and specialist review.
- Ongoing feedback between patients and their healthcare providers.

**Brand promise:**

Your blood pressure journey doesn't end when you leave the clinic. MoyoDoc helps keep your care moving.

**Brand personality:**

- Trustworthy and medically credible.
- Human, warm and reassuring.
- Modern and technology-enabled.
- Accessible and understandable.
- Empowering rather than frightening.
- African in context, globally competitive in execution.

### 3. VISUAL IDENTITY AND DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a premium, modern digital healthcare experience.

**Suggested colour palette:**

- Deep burgundy: #7A1020 — primary brand colour.
- Rich wine: #5B0C19 — headings and emphasis.
- Warm coral: #E97870 — secondary accents and highlights.
- Soft blush: #F9EEEE — section backgrounds.
- Warm white: #FFFCFA — primary background.
- Deep charcoal: #25272B — body text.
- Muted grey: #747780 — secondary text.
- Soft green: #3B8068 — positive health indicators and supporting accents.

**Typography:**

- Headings: Manrope, bold/semi-bold.
- Body: Inter, regular/medium.
- Large, confident headlines.
- Short paragraphs and generous whitespace.

**Design language:**

- Rounded cards and buttons.
- Subtle shadows.
- Clean, spacious layouts.
- Minimal line icons.
- Soft gradients using burgundy and blush.
- Subtle animations and hover interactions.
- Mobile-first responsive design.

Avoid the traditional hospital website appearance. The platform should feel like a sophisticated digital health product that patients can trust and specialists would be proud to use.

### 5. INTERACTIVE ELEMENTS AND USER JOURNEYS

The homepage must not be a static marketing page. Its CTAs should connect to the appropriate product workflows.

**PATIENT JOURNEY**

Homepage → Check My Heart Health → Patient registration → Consent → Cardiovascular intake → Risk summary → Specialist matching → Request specialist enrollment → Specialist acceptance → Patient dashboard.

**PATIENT DASHBOARD PREVIEW**

Show a realistic interface containing:

- My blood pressure trends.
- My medication check-in.
- My weekly symptom check-in.
- My specialist connection.
- My latest care-plan update.

**HEALTHCARE PROVIDER JOURNEY**

Homepage → Join Provider Network → Provider registration → Submission confirmation → Provider verification/onboarding → Practice account setup → Patient enrollment → Clinician dashboard.

**PROVIDER DASHBOARD PREVIEW**

Show:

- Total enrolled patients.
- Out-of-control patient cohort.
- Disengaged / drop-out risk cohort.
- Stable patient cohort.
- Blood pressure trend indicators.
- Alert queue.
- Patient history and documented actions.

Use illustrative/demo data clearly labelled as sample data.

### 6. CONVERSION AND UX REQUIREMENTS

- Keep the patient CTA visible throughout the page.
- Use a persistent but unobtrusive Get Started button.
- Give healthcare providers a clear, dedicated conversion pathway.
- Make registration mobile-friendly.
- Use clear, non-technical language.
- Keep forms short and break longer intake into steps.
- Provide clear feedback after every form submission.
- Make the website responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Use accessible colour contrast and readable font sizes.
- Include SEO-friendly headings and descriptive page metadata.

**Suggested SEO title:**

MoyoDoc Kenya | Connected Hypertension & Cardiovascular Care

**Suggested SEO description:**

Manage hypertension with MoyoDoc. Connect with cardiovascular specialists, track blood pressure readings and stay engaged with your care beyond clinic visits.

**Suggested primary keywords:**

Hypertension management Kenya, cardiovascular care Kenya, blood pressure monitoring, cardiologists in Kenya, hypertension specialist, digital health Kenya.

### 7. IMPORTANT PRODUCT AND CONTENT GUARDRAILS

1. Do not position MoyoDoc as a generic fitness or wellness app.
2. Do not promise that the platform cures or prevents hypertension.
3. Do not claim that the app provides emergency medical services.
4. Do not imply that an automated risk summary is a medical diagnosis.
5. Do not fabricate patient testimonials, clinical outcomes, specialist numbers or partner hospitals.
6. Do not claim that the platform is already certified or fully integrated with hospital EHR systems.
7. Do not advertise AI-powered diagnosis, video consultations or in-app chat as available features.
8. Use the Kenyan market as the initial launch context while communicating a broader African vision.

### 8. FINAL DESIGN INSTRUCTION

Create a polished, premium, conversion-focused digital health website that feels credible to a Kenyan cardiologist and welcoming to an ordinary Kenyan patient.

The visual hierarchy must prioritize the patient call to action while making the healthcare provider pathway equally visible.

The homepage should communicate one idea repeatedly and consistently:

**MoyoDoc makes hypertension care continuous, visible and actionable.**

Deliver:

- Complete responsive homepage.
- Desktop and mobile layouts.
- Actual copy as specified above.
- Functional navigation and CTA links.
- Patient and provider registration entry points.
- App and clinician dashboard preview sections.
- Reusable components and consistent design tokens.
- Accessible, production-ready UI.

Do not generate placeholder marketing copy where actual copy is provided in this specification. Use the supplied wording as the starting content.
